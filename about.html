My story

My first job was as a service crew member at the Clan-Anne Canteen and Catering Services. There I learned how to run a clean, orderly workspace, and to check stock before it runs out.

Later I worked remotely in customer support for KaizenVapes, researching products and keeping listings accurate on Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Shopify, and WordPress. That job taught me how small data errors become customer problems, and how to catch them early.

I then completed my degree at STI West Negros University, where I did my on-the-job training as an IT Support Assistant. I repaired desktop and printer hardware, installed LAN cabling, and kept campus computer labs running during busy academic weeks.
